# Map: Booth NPC Lead Recommender

Label: `wayfinder:map`

## Destination

A **build-ready spec** at `.scratch/booth-npc-app/spec.md` for the UOB booth experience: an NFC-launched, pixel-art NPC conversation on the visitor's phone that captures contacts, qualifies and scores leads, routes them to a BU, and feeds a live booth-staff view plus an organiser funnel dashboard.

The map is done when someone can build the whole thing from the spec without another design conversation. **Writing the app is not in this map.**

> ## ✅ Destination reached — 2026-08-31
>
> All 19 tickets closed. The spec is at [`spec.md`](spec.md). Nothing remains to decide before someone goes and builds it.
>
> Two things sit outside the spec and still need doing before the event: the **provisioning checklist** (§17 of the spec — four names, two-person credential cover, two tablets), and a **DPO review** of the three PDPA points flagged in §7. Neither is a design decision, which is why neither blocked this map.

## Notes

**Domain.** UOB convention booth. Sanctioned internal project, real booth, real visitor PII. Convention is **under 4 weeks away** — prefer boring, fast, reversible choices; cut anything not needed to capture leads on the day.

**Skills every session should consult:** `/grilling` and `/domain-modeling` by default. `/prototype` for the two prototype tickets. `/research` (subagent) for research tickets. Read `CONTEXT.md` at the repo root for vocabulary before writing any ticket answer.

**Assets are fixed** — the four files in `Game Assets/` are the entire art budget:

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `Background Screen.jpg` | base layer, NPC receptionist at the UOB booth |
| `Dialog Interface.jpg` | **reference only** — the frame is rebuilt in CSS (ticket 11); a flat image cannot hold per-node copy |
| `Dialog Example.jpg` | populated reference for type, colour and choice styling |
| `Contact Detail Form.jpg` | check-in modal reference |

Everything else — the dialogue frame and choice panel included — is CSS/HTML in the same pixel idiom. **Ticket 11 found that only `Background Screen.jpg` is actually rendered as an image**; the other three are references to reproduce. That helps ticket 08's ≤1.2 MB cold load, since three of the four JPGs never need downloading.

**Source brief.** The user's original brief (booth journey, progressive profiling, scoring table, BU routing, funnel) is the input document, **now committed at `.scratch/booth-npc-app/brief.md`** with sections 1–18 and the original numbering intact. Treat it as a *draft* — several tickets exist precisely because parts of it don't survive contact with implementation.

**Spine decided during charting** (do not re-open without cause):

- Scripted decision tree, not an LLM. Deterministic, offline-capable, no improvising bank NPC.
- Standalone app; no UOB InfoSec approval gate.
- A small hosted DB is the source of truth; a **cloud Excel workbook is a synced export**, not the live store.
- Both dashboards are in scope: the **live booth staff view** and the **organiser funnel dashboard**.
- **Footfall = NFC tap / app open.** One URL on every tag; each open mints a session id.
- Handoff is **live staff alert at the booth** + **post-event routed export** to BU owners.
- Build **offline-tolerant**: whole conversation runs client-side, submission queues and retries.
- Gamification is the **JRPG skin only** — no points, prizes or leaderboard.
- The spec carries **full NPC dialogue copy**, so the build is assembly.
- **PDPA is specified properly**: consent wording, purpose, retention, deletion path.
- The brief's generic BU names are **placeholders**; the real UOB BU list and owners are not ready.

## Decisions so far

<!-- one line per closed ticket: gist + link -->

- [Stack, hosting and the NFC URL](issues/01-stack-hosting-and-the-nfc-url.md) — Next.js on Vercel with Supabase Postgres as source of truth and Realtime for the Staff View; one deployment with `/`, `/staff`, `/dashboard` route groups; the visitor route is client-rendered with the Node graph bundled as static JSON; a pinned Vercel platform subdomain on the tags, with the trust mitigations and a write-the-tags-late hedge recorded; one shared event PIN gating both internal surfaces.
- [Cloud Excel export mechanism](issues/02-cloud-excel-export-mechanism.md) — the Graph workbook API does not support app-only auth at all (`EditModeAccessDenied`), ruling out OneDrive/SharePoint without a fragile person-owned refresh token and a tenant; recommendation is to skip live spreadsheets entirely and generate a real `.xlsx` server-side into Supabase Storage on a Vercel Cron plus an on-demand button, full-rewrite each time so there is no append contention. Google Sheets via service account remains the fallback if a live browsable sheet is wanted.

- [PDPA consent, retention and deletion](issues/07-pdpa-consent-retention-and-deletion.md) — narrow consent (this conversation only, not marketing) on the check-in form, unticked, gating Submit so no unconsented personal data is ever transmitted; Address dropped and Phone made optional behind its own DNC channel consent; consent stored as an evidential record rather than a boolean; 90-day retention then hard delete, with an active-lead-or-customer carve-out adopted later so the job cannot delete a Lead a BU is actively pursuing; withdrawal by booth staff on the spot, with controller, withdrawal contact and privacy-notice URL left as named placeholders in [Controller, withdrawal contact and privacy notice URL](issues/17-privacy-notice-facts.md).

- [Offline behaviour and submission durability](issues/08-offline-behaviour-and-submission-durability.md) — a service worker precaches the app shell and all four assets (re-encoded to WebP for a ≤ 1.2 MB cold load), so the conversation survives a reload on dead wifi; submission is a **single POST at the CTA**, chosen against the recommendation, which means an abandoned session leaves no trace at all and the Staff View shows nothing mid-conversation; the payload queues in `localStorage` with exponential backoff, Background Sync where available (never on iOS) and a flush on next app open; failures are silent to the visitor; and Footfall is now a **mandatory PII-free beacon** fired on app open, since Lead rows no longer exist for anyone who does not finish.

- [Event-day operations and deploy freeze](issues/15-event-day-operations-and-deploy-freeze.md) — hard freeze during floor hours (30 min either side of doors) with deploys batched to an overnight window and verified before reopening, but **rollback explicitly carved out as permitted at any time** since reverting to the verified build reduces risk rather than adding it; a one-route hotfix path whose smoke test must run on a deliberately *dirty* phone with the old service worker still installed; a pre-cached staff tablet as the outage fallback, preserving the full scored conversation rather than degrading to a name and email; monitoring left to the Staff View, which therefore now owes a last-lead-received timestamp and a Realtime connection indicator; every credential held by at least two people; and the 90-day deletion job plus the staff consent-withdrawal duty both inherited from ticket 07.

- [Rehearsal plan and load check](issues/18-rehearsal-plan-and-load-check.md) — a two-stage rehearsal: a full office run at T-7 with real staff and four real devices (current *and* older iPhone and Android, since the older pair carries the real risk), then an on-site smoke test on build day for the things only the hall can prove — network, tap behaviour, legibility under convention lighting. The tag deadline tension dissolves rather than trades: **write two or three sample tags now, the bulk batch late**, which keeps ticket 01's hedge intact. Load work is ruled out by arithmetic (~0.23 writes/second at a 10x peak); the real risks are the hall network, Vercel cold starts and nothing to do with database throughput. Rehearsal must exercise the iOS next-open queue flush, the fallback tablet in anger, and the dirty-service-worker procedure — each of which is otherwise attempted for the first time under pressure. **Implies the build is feature-complete a week before the convention.**

- [Commit the source brief to the repo](issues/16-commit-the-source-brief.md) — supplied by the user on 2026-08-31 at `.scratch/booth-npc-app/brief.md`; it had existed only in chat history and was not recoverable by searching the machine. Carries sections 1–18, including four (13, 14, 16, 18) that no ticket had referenced because nobody could see them — band thresholds, CTA-per-band, and a worked staff-view mock-up.
- [The conversation node graph](issues/03-the-conversation-node-graph.md) — twelve nodes on a linear spine (`checkin → interest → need.* → timeline → intent → cta`), whose only branch is the Interest choosing one of seven `need` nodes; each of the brief's multi-question chains folds into a single `need` node, with the §12 scoring scale winning as the one shared `timeline` because it is the only scale that has to compute. **Surfaced that the scoring table does not compute as written** — Engagement is scored from the CTA choice while the CTA is selected from the Band, closing a loop that forces a two-phase score — and that routing has one genuine fall-through plus one orphan BU row. Two of the brief's own lead-quality signals (FX frequency, current tooling) are lost to uniform depth and are recorded rather than quietly dropped.

- [Make the lead score computable](issues/04-make-the-lead-score-computable.md) — a **two-phase score**, forced rather than chosen: a provisional score (max 90) picks which CTA is emphasised, the visitor's choice adds Engagement, and the final score sets the Band that staff and routing see. §12's two Need rows merge into one 25-point category (the +15 fired for everyone under uniform depth, and "pain point" had no input anywhere); Company Fit is repurposed from an impossible target-company list to corporate-versus-free email domain; role is always present, so no missing-data rule is needed. §14's four-CTAs-but-three-buttons contradiction resolves as **four wordings across three actions**. Incomplete sessions are capped at EXPLORATORY. All four bands verified reachable with worked examples, one landing within a point of the brief's own figure.

- [BU routing table and function](issues/05-bu-routing-table-and-function.md) — §15's slashed pairs collapse to **eight canonical placeholder BUs**, and routing is a flat 40-row lookup keyed on the need option alone (the `(interest, need)` pair is fully determined by it), proven total against the graph's 40 options with a test asserting the two key sets match. **One primary BU per lead**, with a `secondary_bu` column present but null so ranked pairs stay a config change; four natural secondaries recorded for when it is switched on. §15's unreachable *Acquisition / M&A* row is given a home via `need.financing.specific_investment`, and the two declared non-needs route to a real `other_unrouted` BU that is **excluded from the export** — no owner, nobody chasing. **Headline popularity is the Interest split**, matching §17's own table, with routed BU as a second view and a standing caveat that the scrolling panel biases it by option order. The retail-signage-versus-corporate-questions mismatch was assessed and changes no routing — it is a tone question, handed to tickets 11 and 13.

- [Data model and the contact record](issues/06-data-model-and-the-contact-record.md) — three tables: a PII-free `sessions` row per Tap, an **append-only** `leads` row per completed session, and `consent_versions` holding each wording once so consent is evidential without duplicating the sentence on every row (new version on edit, never an update). Answers are four typed columns, the tension dissolved by uniform depth; score, band and BU are **frozen** because the visitor was shown a CTA chosen from that band, with inputs and a `scoring_version` retained so bugs are recomputable without rewriting history. Duplicate taps each write their own row, deduped downstream by highest score, so a casual re-tap cannot destroy a hot lead. **Caught that Company Name had been dropped by accident** from tickets 07 and 03 — both corrected. Two properties worth keeping: `sessions` holds no personal data so the funnel survives the 90-day deletion intact, and §17's *Engaged* stage is currently unmeasurable, with a second PII-free beacon specified as the cheap fix for ticket 10 to confirm.

- [Organiser funnel dashboard](issues/10-organiser-funnel-dashboard.md) — five stages, each a count the system genuinely observes: Taps, Engaged (restored by a **second PII-free beacon** at the Interest node), Contactable Leads, Qualified (score ≥ 40), and BU Handoffs. The funnel stays session-level per `CONTEXT.md`, with a separate Unique Contacts figure so nobody has to guess whether a number means runs or people. §17's CRM stages below Handoffs are **drawn greyed and labelled 'not tracked here'** rather than omitted, because a blank invites someone to read it as zero. Live during the event on a 60-second poll rather than Realtime, deliberately keeping load off the channel ticket 15 uses as its only monitoring signal. Cuts are time-only. **Corrected ticket 05's denominator:** the Interest split is counted over Leads, not over engaged sessions, because the beacon records that the node was reached and never which option was picked.

- [Post-event routed export to BU owners](issues/12-post-event-routed-export.md) — **one `.xlsx` per BU, never one file for everyone**, because forwarding a master workbook would disclose every other BU's prospects to each owner for no upside. Every lead for that BU is included regardless of band, sorted by score with Band as the first column, so the BU sets its own bar rather than having ours imposed. Purpose consent needs no export filter (it gated the write), but **phone consent is enforced here as an empty cell** — a BU owner cannot call a number they were never given, which makes the DNC position structural. Generated by a button behind the event PIN and forwarded by a human, because automated mail would ship dead config against a mailbox list ruled out of scope. Packs are cumulative and generated at the close of **every** event day, which turns out to satisfy §13's 24-hour promise for the whole event rather than just the early days. Surfaced that the 90-day deletion covered only the database, leaving exported packs of prospect PII in storage indefinitely — now purged too, with a cover sheet carrying the constraints on copies that travel beyond our control.

- [Visitor UI: screen inventory and pixel style](issues/11-visitor-ui-screen-inventory-and-pixel-style.md) — **Variant A (overlay) on every screen, set in Press Start 2P**, chosen from a three-variant prototype ([local](prototypes/visitor-ui-prototype.html) · [published](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/c618ed11-e261-4842-a6b5-b4a9e0d2a767)). Because Press Start 2P is a fixed-advance face, this fixes an exact copy budget rather than an estimate: **3 lines × ~26 characters ≈ 78 per node, and ~22 characters per option label** — the hard input ticket 13 was waiting for, and it means three existing option labels need shortening. Two useful side effects: the overlay's crop pushes the Priority Banking / Wealth Management signage largely out of frame, **mitigating ticket 05's tone mismatch by composition rather than by copy**; and only `Background Screen.jpg` ships as an image at all — the dialogue frame, choice panel and contact form are CSS reproductions, since a flat JPG cannot hold copy that changes length per node.

- [Full NPC dialogue copy](issues/13-full-npc-dialogue-copy.md) — the complete script: every node in both registers, all option labels, check-in and validation strings, the four CTA endings and the edge copy. Voice is **warm but brief**, acknowledging each answer with one short beat, and **never using the visitor's name** — no first-name extraction, so no mangled names in front of a Singapore audience. **The budget turned out not to bind:** the longest line is 51 characters against 78, so ticket 11's fallback levers are unnecessary and the tight Overlay + Press Start 2P pairing is safe. Two findings beyond the copy itself: the exit label sat one row under the *Just Exploring* Interest reading almost identically, a coin-flip between continuing and ending — renamed **Skip to the end**; and the CTA headline is held **identical across all four bands**, since a headline that warmed for HOT and cooled for EXPLORATORY would tell the visitor how they had been graded. Audited line by line against BU names, scores, qualification language and ticket 01's credential-shaped-ask ban: all clear.

- [Live booth staff view](issues/09-live-booth-staff-view.md) — **Variant C, triage split, on staff phones** ([local](prototypes/staff-view-prototype.html) · [published](https://claude.ai/code/artifact/63bf7398-9578-4bec-8a6e-4b2b185a79e3)): unhandled leads as rich cards, handled collapsed, so the working list visibly shrinks. HOT arrives as a **silent banner pinned until acknowledged** — floor noise cannot defeat it — and acknowledging is deliberately *not* the same as handled, or the Handoffs stage would count leads nobody spoke to. Two decisions specific to a booth: the queue sorts by **recency, not band**, because presence decays with time while band does not (a 40-minute-old HOT lead has left; a 90-second-old WARM is ten feet away); and a **30-minute ageing rule** moves untouched leads to Earlier, without which the working list grows to hundreds by mid-afternoon and destroys the very property that made C the right choice. Choosing phones broke ticket 15's monitoring premise — repaired by a **dedicated counter tablet** running this screen purely as a monitor, making two distinct tablets a hard requirement.

- [Controller, withdrawal contact and privacy notice URL](issues/17-privacy-notice-facts.md) — all three found on UOB's own published pages rather than requested: **United Overseas Bank Limited** (Co. Reg. 193500026Z), **dataprotectionofficer@uobgroup.com**, and the **Corporate** privacy notice. Every placeholder across the tickets and prototypes is now filled. The notice URL was fetched and read, not just linked — which changed the assessment: it is not merely *broader* than what we collect, it states it *"forms a part of the terms and conditions governing your relationship with United Overseas Bank"* and covers data of shareholders and directors for *"products and services which you have applied for"*. A booth visitor has no relationship and has applied for nothing, so it describes a situation that does not exist for them. Linking it is defensible and the narrow consent line governs the purpose, but a short event-specific notice remains the cleaner fix and this is squarely what the DPO review should look at. Also caught that the DPO **cannot delete from this app** — a forwarding step to the data owner is now a named runbook duty, or the contact is a signposted dead end.

- [Assemble the spec](issues/14-assemble-the-spec.md) — **the destination, reached.** [`spec.md`](spec.md) is 878 lines across 18 sections, compiled from 17 resolved tickets, two prototypes and the brief, and stands alone for a reader who has seen none of them. The out-of-scope audit this ticket required was performed and is clean: every ruled-out item appears only in the exclusion table or as an explicit ruling-out. Two things were added that no single ticket owned — a consolidated **Open items** section so a builder meets the gaps in one place, and an **appendix of the nine decisions that overrode the brief**, so a reader comparing the two does not mistake a deliberate departure for an oversight.

- **"Explore Solutions" content.** The ⚪ EXPLORATORY ending needs somewhere to send people. Depends on what UOB collateral or links actually exist.
- **Drop-off instrumentation.** Per-node abandonment analytics, beyond the headline funnel. **Ticket 10 considered per-node beacons and declined them** — they capture no extra leads, and the map's standing instruction is to cut anything not needed on the day. One beacon was added at the Interest node, so tap-to-start drop-off is now visible; abandonment *within* the four nodes remains unobservable by choice, not by oversight. Revisit only if a second event wants the curve.
- **Accessibility of a pixel-art dialogue UI.** Contrast against the background art, text scaling, tap targets, screen-reader story. The prototype has landed, and the choice made it **more** pressing, not less: Press Start 2P at 10.5px in a scrolling panel is the least forgiving option that was on the table. Ticket 18 now checks legibility on the budget Android under hall lighting, but the screen-reader and text-scaling story is still unwritten.
- **Language.** English-only assumed for a Singapore audience; still unconfirmed — and now **concrete**, since ticket 13 has written the entire deck in English. A second language would mean a second full deck against the same 78-character budget, not a translation pass, because the budget is per-line.
- **Edge and error states.** Duplicate email, two visitors sharing a phone, back-button mid-tree, resuming an abandoned session.
- **Life after the convention.** Whether this is reused at a second event. *(The data question is now answered: 90-day retention then hard delete, per ticket 07.)*
