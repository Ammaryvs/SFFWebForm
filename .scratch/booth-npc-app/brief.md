The overall principle should be:

> **Capture → Understand → Qualify → Connect**

# 2. Stage 1 — Quick Check-In

Don't start with a bunch of questions.

The booth staff can say:

> **"Hi! Welcome. Are you here to explore any business solutions today?"**

If yes, begin the quick registration.

### Basic Information

Only collect:

1. **Name**
2. **Company Name**
3. **Company Email**

Then optionally:

4. **Job Title / Role**
5. **Existing customer?**

### Why these matter

| Information     | Why collect it                                  |
| --------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Name            | Identify lead                                   |
| Company         | Identify organisation and potential account     |
| Email           | Follow-up                                       |
| Role            | Determine influence / decision-making authority |
| Customer status | Existing vs prospect                            |

**Important:** Don't ask all five verbally if you can avoid it. Let the visitor enter the first three themselves on a tablet/QR form.

---

# 3. Stage 2 — The Conversation Starter

Instead of:

> "Which BU are you interested in?"

Ask something more natural:

### **"What's most relevant to your business at the moment?"**

Give them 5–6 broad options.

**🚀 Growing**

> Expanding, entering new markets or growing the business

**💰 Managing Cash**

> Cash flow, liquidity and working capital

**💳 Payments**

> Making or receiving payments more efficiently

**🏦 Financing**

> Funding growth, working capital or investments

**🌏 Managing Risk**

> FX, interest rates and treasury

**⚙️ Digitalising**

> Automating financial processes and improving efficiency

**💡 Just Exploring**

> Want to see what's available

This single question gives you your **BU/interest distribution** without making visitors feel like they're being interviewed.

---

# 4. Progressive Profiling

This is the most important part.

**Don't ask everyone the same questions.**

The next question depends on what they selected.

---

## A. 🚀 Growing / Expanding

Start with:

> **"Interesting — are you mainly looking at growing locally or expanding overseas?"**

### If overseas:

> "Which market are you looking at?"

Options:

* ASEAN
* Greater China
* Australia / New Zealand
* Europe
* US
* Other

Then:

> **"Is this something you're actively planning, or still exploring?"**

* Already planning
* Within 3 months
* 3–12 months
* Just exploring

### Lead signal

Someone saying:

> "We're expanding into Malaysia next quarter."

is significantly more valuable than:

> "I'm just curious about overseas expansion."

---

# 5. 💰 Cash Flow / Working Capital

Start with:

> **"What would you most like to improve — visibility, liquidity or access to working capital?"**

Then only ask another question if appropriate.

### If "visibility":

> "Is it difficult today to get a clear view of your cash across different accounts or entities?"

### If "liquidity":

> "Are you mainly looking to optimise excess cash or improve access to funds?"

### If "working capital":

> "Is this something you're looking at in the near term?"

* Now
* 1–3 months
* 3–6 months
* Just exploring

---

# 6. 💳 Payments & Collections

Start with:

> **"Are you mainly looking to improve payments, collections, or both?"**

### Payments

Follow-up:

> "What's the biggest challenge today?"

* Too manual
* Too many platforms
* Cross-border payments
* Reconciliation
* Processing time
* Cost

### Collections

Follow-up:

> "Are you mainly looking to get paid faster or improve visibility/reconciliation?"

Then:

> **"Would solving this be a priority in the next few months?"**

---

# 7. 🏦 Financing

Start with:

> **"Are you looking at financing for growth, working capital or a specific investment?"**

Depending on their answer:

### Growth

> "Are you already planning the expansion?"

### Working capital

> "Is the need immediate or something you're planning for?"

### Investment

> "What are you looking to fund?"

Then:

> **"When are you looking to secure the financing?"**

* Immediately
* 1–3 months
* 3–6 months
* 6–12 months
* Just exploring

This is a **very strong lead qualification question**.

---

# 8. 🌏 FX / Treasury / Risk

Start with:

> **"Does your business deal with foreign currencies regularly?"**

If yes:

> "Is your main concern managing FX exposure, improving rates, or managing volatility?"

Then:

> **"How frequently does this come up for your business?"**

* Daily / frequently
* Monthly
* Occasionally
* Rarely

High-frequency FX exposure + clear pain point = strong lead.

---

# 9. ⚙️ Digitalisation / Automation

Start with:

> **"Which part of your financial processes would you most like to make easier?"**

* Payments
* Collections
* Reconciliation
* Cash management
* Reporting
* Trade
* Other

Then:

> **"How are you currently managing this?"**

* Manually
* Excel
* Multiple systems
* Existing banking platform
* ERP / integrated system

Then only for promising leads:

> **"Are you currently looking at changing or improving this process?"**

This distinguishes **general curiosity from an active digitalisation project**.

---

# 10. "Just Exploring" Visitors

Don't interrogate them.

Ask just **one** question:

> **"Sure! Is there a particular area of your business you're curious about?"**

If they identify something → route them into that BU.

If not:

> **"No problem — would you like us to send you some information on what we offer?"**

Then finish.

This keeps the experience positive while still capturing the visitor.

---

# 11. The Final Progressive Step — Intent

Once you've identified a genuine need, don't ask five more questions.

Ask one powerful question:

### **"How are you looking to address this today?"**

* 🔥 **Actively looking for a solution**
* 🟠 **Considering options**
* 🟡 **Just researching**

This is more conversational than:

> "How likely are you to purchase?"

# 12. Lead Scoring System

I'd use **100 points**, with heavier weighting on actual intent.

| Category               | Condition                      | Points |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------ | -----: |
| **Need**               | Clear business need identified |    +15 |
|                        | Specific pain point identified |    +10 |
| **Timeline**           | Immediate / <1 month           |    +20 |
|                        | 1–3 months                     |    +15 |
|                        | 3–6 months                     |    +10 |
|                        | 6–12 months                    |     +5 |
| **Intent**             | Actively looking for solution  |    +20 |
|                        | Considering options            |    +10 |
|                        | Researching                    |     +5 |
| **Decision Influence** | Decision maker                 |    +15 |
|                        | Influencer / relevant function |    +10 |
|                        | General employee               |     +5 |
| **Company Fit**        | Strategic / target company     |    +10 |
| **Engagement**         | Agrees to BU follow-up         |    +10 |

### Maximum: 100 points

---

# 13. Lead Classification

### 🔥 80–100 — HOT

**Definition:**

Clear need + strong intent + near-term timeline.

Example:

> CFO of a regional company looking for working capital financing within 2 months.

### Action

**Immediate BU handoff**

CTA:

> **"Would you like me to connect you with our specialist?"**

Target: **same day / within 24 hours**

---

### 🟠 60–79 — WARM

Clear need, but timing or intent isn't immediate.

CTA:

> **"Would you like our specialist to follow up with you?"**

Target: **2–3 working days**

---

### 🟡 40–59 — QUALIFIED / NURTURE

Relevant business need but low urgency.

CTA:

> **"Would you like us to send you some information on this?"**

Add to appropriate BU nurture journey.

---

### ⚪ 0–39 — EXPLORATORY

Visitor is primarily browsing.

CTA:

> **"Would you like to explore some of our solutions?"**

No aggressive follow-up.

---

# 14. CTA Should Match Their Intent

Don't give everyone the same CTA.

| Lead         | CTA                      |
| ------------ | ------------------------ |
| 🔥 Hot       | **Talk to a Specialist** |
| 🟠 Warm      | **Request a Follow-Up**  |
| 🟡 Qualified | **Send Me Information**  |
| ⚪ Exploring  | **Explore Solutions**    |

You can make the final screen very simple:

> ### How can we help you from here?
>
> **👤 Talk to a Specialist**
>
> **📩 Send Me Information**
>
> **🔎 Explore More Solutions**

Their selection itself becomes a **lead-scoring signal**.

# 15. BU Routing

Behind the scenes, map every response to a BU.

| Visitor Need       | Primary BU                                |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| Business expansion | Business Banking / relevant coverage team |
| Working capital    | Financing / Business Banking              |
| Cash visibility    | Cash Management                           |
| Payments           | Transaction Banking / Payments            |
| Collections        | Transaction Banking / Cash Management     |
| FX                 | Markets / Treasury                        |
| Interest rate risk | Markets / Treasury                        |
| Digital payments   | Digital / Transaction Banking             |
| Process automation | Digital / Transaction Banking             |
| Trade              | Trade Finance                             |
| Acquisition / M&A  | Corporate / Investment Banking            |

The **visitor doesn't need to know the BU name**.

They tell you:

> "We're struggling with cross-border collections."

Your system determines:

> **BU = Transaction Banking / Cash Management**

# 18. The Core Design Principle

The booth shouldn't feel like:

> **"Please complete this 15-question survey."**

It should feel like:

> **"Tell us what's happening in your business, and we'll point you towards the right solution."**

So the experience becomes:

**1. Who are you?**
→ Name + Company + Email

**2. What's happening in your business?**
→ Broad interest

**3. Tell me a little more.**
→ One relevant follow-up

**4. How urgent is it?**
→ Timeline / intent

**5. How can we help?**
→ Specialist / information / explore

That gives you **footfall + customer information + BU popularity + qualified leads + BU routing + measurable business opportunities**, while keeping the visitor experience conversational rather than survey-like.
