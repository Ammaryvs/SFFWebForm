What the prototype is testing

Required by ticket 15

Staff must recognise the broken state

The connection dot and the last lead received clock are not decoration — with no dead-man's switch, they are the only way anyone learns that capture has stopped.

Drop connection and Quiet for 20 min show staff the two failure states. Ticket 18 requires rehearsing these, because a person cannot recognise a state they have never seen.

Required by 07, 10 and 12

"Handled" is load-bearing, not a to-do tick

Marking a lead handled feeds the dashboard's BU Handoffs stage, and it sets the retention exemption that stops the 90-day job deleting a lead a BU is still pursuing.

So the control has to be easy to hit and hard to hit by accident — it is doing data-protection work.

Found by building it

The working list has to age out

Around 300 leads a day arrive and staff will not mark every one handled — they are talking to people. Without ageing, Needs action grows all day until it is a scroll of hundreds, most of them visitors who left hours ago.

So a lead leaves the working list when it is handled or once it is 30 minutes old. Nothing is lost — it moves to Earlier and still reaches its BU in the nightly pack. Press Busy afternoon to see the difference.

From ticket 04

The score explains itself

Every lead carries its per-category breakdown, because "why did this one score 65?" is the first thing a sceptical salesperson asks. Tap a lead to see it.

Bands and BU names are fine here — no visitor ever sees this screen.
